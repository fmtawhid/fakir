SCEOL: Scalable Contribution Economy Law

Alongside Bengal Protocol and AgroMind, FM Shihab has authored SCEOL—a groundbreaking economic theory that redefines how we measure value in the AI era.

The SCEOL Formula

C = (Wb + We) × T × S

Where C = Contribution, Wb = Human Work, We = AI Enhancement, T = Time, S = Skill

Unlike traditional economics that views AI as a replacement for human labor, SCEOL proves that artificial intelligence amplifies human capability. When integrated into blockchain and smart contracts, SCEOL enables fair value distribution across decentralized networks—a cornerstone for Web4 and the future economy.

Learn about SCEOL Applications →

Economic Theory Meets Practice

How SCEOL transforms business and society

Contribution Zero Rule

If any core element—work, time, or skill—equals zero, total contribution becomes zero. This ensures quality and accountability in AI-assisted systems.

AI Amplification

Technology amplifies human output without replacing human judgment. The same skill + more AI support = exponentially better results.

Scalable Measurement

Works across individuals, teams, and organizations. Enables transparent contribution tracking in DAOs and decentralized networks.

Real-World Applications

SCEOL is implemented across our ecosystem: In Shihab Law's legal technology, measuring attorney-AI collaboration. In Bengal Protocol's validator rewards, ensuring fair compensation for network participation. In AgroMind's farmer partnerships, tracking and valuing farmer-AI agricultural optimization. In Fakir Ventures' portfolio management, assessing founder-ecosystem contribution.

"SCEOL proves that the future isn't about humans versus AI. It's about humans empowered by AI, creating exponentially greater value."
