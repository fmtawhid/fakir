Vision & Leadership

FM Shihab is the visionary founder of Fakir Ventures and the driving force behind transforming Bangladesh into a global hub for AI-powered innovation. His leadership combines deep entrepreneurial insight with economic theory, creating a unique approach to building technology-driven enterprises.

As an active participant in the global blockchain ecosystem and recognized Web3 builder, Shihab has achieved top contributor status on Story Protocol and completed hundreds of on-chain transactions, demonstrating both technical expertise and practical implementation capabilities.

His work revolves around five core pillars: Bengal Protocol, AgroMind Protocol, SCEOL Economic Law, the Fakir Ventures ecosystem, and AI-powered Web3 solutions. Together, these initiatives demonstrate his commitment to building technologies that address real-world challenges and create opportunities for future generations.

Core Initiatives

Five pillars shaping the future

⛓️

Bengal Protocol

Bangladesh's first Layer-1 blockchain designed to support decentralized applications, digital assets, and scalable blockchain infrastructure. Operating with Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) consensus mechanism, 101 validators, and powered by the native $BENGAL token.

Long-term objective: Create a sovereign blockchain ecosystem supporting economic growth, digital identity, governance, and next-generation applications.

🌾

AgroMind Protocol

AI-powered agricultural intelligence platform transforming farming by providing farmers with data-driven insights, decision-support systems, and advanced agricultural technologies.

Impact: 22,000+ farmers using AI-powered tools for improved productivity, reduced risks, and smarter farming practices across Bangladesh.

📊

SCEOL Economic Law

Scalable Contribution Economy Law - a revolutionary economic theory measuring human contribution in the modern AI-powered economy. Unlike traditional models focusing solely on labor, SCEOL calculates contribution based on combined impact of work, AI assistance, time, and skill.

Framework: Introduces principles like Contribution Zero Rule and AI amplification, integrating blockchain, DAOs, and smart contracts for future economies.

🤝

Fakir Foundation & Fakir School

Non-profit arm dedicated to social impact, education, research, and public-good technology projects. Focuses on creating opportunities for underserved communities and supporting initiatives for long-term societal value.

Fakir School: Educational platform making high-quality learning opportunities accessible, especially for Bengali-speaking audiences in technology and entrepreneurship.
